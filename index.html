    Jaroos

    Mix studio · version 1.0

    Jaroos turns a voice note, speech or song into a finished mix. Upload or record up to three tracks, then add backing beats, Indian and world instruments, DJ and voice effects, special sounds and ambience. Record the result and save or share it. Everything runs in your browser, and the installed app works offline.
